## 1. Description & Challenge Role
> *"The CSA's Space Resources for STEM and Youth Organizations page provides free, bilingual tools and activities to help educators engage youth in space and STEM. Designed for museums, libraries, camps, after-school clubs, community groups, and outreach organizations, these resources support learning beyond the classroom."*

---

## 2. Pedagogical Guidelines for the Mission Trainer
The CSA youth guidelines define specific design principles for junior learners (ages 7–12):
1. **Low Barrier to Entry**: Visual meters with color-coding (Green/Yellow/Red) rather than abstract equations.
2. **Bilingual Ready**: Built-in support for English and French localization.
3. **Safe Failure & Learning Loops**: When a habitat runs out of battery or air, the game provides an encouraging "Mission Debrief" explaining what happened and prompt: *"Try again with more solar panels!"*
4. **Gamified Missions**: Badges for achieving milestones (e.g. *"First Hydroponic Harvest"*, *"Solar Storm Survivor"*, *"Zero Waste Recycler"*).
