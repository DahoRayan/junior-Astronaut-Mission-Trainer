## 1. Description & Challenge Role
> *"This web package is a one-stop resource for educators and youth who want to learn about food in space. This toolkit aims to outline how food production in space can relate to the health and nutrition of astronauts as well as benefit humans on Earth."*

---

## 2. Key Concepts & Space Agriculture Mechanics
The CSA toolkit highlights how closed-loop agriculture in extreme environments (Moon/Mars and Earth's Northern Arctic/remote regions) solves shared challenges:
1. **Resource Scarcity**: Extreme conservation of water, LED electricity, and soil nutrients.
2. **Fresh Food & Psychological Well-being**: Astronauts on long missions experience "menu fatigue." Fresh crunchy produce (lettuce, radishes, cherry tomatoes) significantly elevates crew mental health and cognitive morale.
3. **Dual Benefit to Earth**: Technologies developed for lunar food production (vertical farming, low-water hydroponics, automated nutrient delivery) directly help remote northern communities on Earth grow food year-round.

---

## 3. Simulator Implementation
* **Hydroponics Tray Manager**: Players plant and manage crop trays (lettuce, radish, tomatoes, microgreens).
* **Morale Bonus**: Consuming freshly harvested produce provides a $+10\%$ crew morale boost and slows down psychological fatigue.
* **Water & Power Cost**: Each active food tray requires continuous LED light power and recycled water.
